# Combat Punchy

Combat Punchy switches you into Epic Fight combat when you attack with a supported melee weapon or take a hit from another entity. It can stow weapons on your back and hand off animations between Epic Fight, Punchy and Punchy! Moves.

| Minecraft | Loader | Required Epic Fight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.21.1 | [NeoForge 21.1.262 or later in the 21.1 series](https://github.com/Mikaaah/Combat-Punchy/tree/neoforge-1.21.1) | 21.17.3.1 |
| 1.20.1 | [Forge 47.4.13 or later in the 47 series](https://github.com/Mikaaah/Combat-Punchy/tree/forge-1.20.1) | 20.14.17 |

Punchy (3.2a to below 3.3) and Punchy! Moves (1.0.9 or newer) are optional on both loaders.
